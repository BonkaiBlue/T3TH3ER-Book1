# TETHER — Book One: story bible (Part One)

**Logline.** Specimen Nine, the ninth clone of a ruthless mercenary called *The Snare*, escapes the lab that made him,
learns to swing through Brasshaven on gauntlets built by the scientist who secretly gave him a conscience, and has to
decide who to be when every villain insists he's only a copy.

## Cast
- **Tether / Specimen Nine** — the clone. Template body, brand-new choices. Dry, curious, terrified of becoming his original.
- **Dr. Imelda Okafor** — Foundry geneticist who designed the line gauntlets and recorded tapes inside them. Dies in the Foundry fire; her tapes are the book's emotional spine.
- **Baron Ferrous** — iron-masked industrialist who builds weapons, machines and people. Speaks only through intercoms and radios in Part One.
- **Rook Malloy, "The Snare"** — Nine's template, a trapper-mercenary. Off-screen in Part One; back in Ferrous's employ (revealed Ch.4).
- **Pip Albright** — 12-year-old pigeon keeper on a brownstone roof. Names him "Tether". Gives him a skateboard and a police scanner.
- **Officer Dolores Reyes** — Midtown beat cop; skeptical ally ("a problem I can use").
- **Gus Pappas** — pawnshop owner, first person Tether saves.
- **Augie Bellweather** — editor of *The Evening Klaxon*; calls Tether a menace on air.
- **The Violet Jester** — vaudeville crime boss on a jet-kite; hired by Ferrous to "stress-test" Nine.

## Chapters
1. **Spark** — Wake, fire, Okafor pinned by a beam ("Who you are hasn't happened yet"). Movement tutorial through the burning Foundry; first takedowns in the yard; Locker 7 holds the gauntlets. Ferrous: *"Every string leads back to the hand that holds it."*
2. **Line & Self** — Okafor's tapes teach swinging and point-launching, then tell the truth about Malloy. Clock-tower contemplation. Pip names him Tether.
3. **Hero Work** — Scanner calls: stealth robbery at Gus's, Reyes' first meeting, a park mugging, a getaway van. The Klaxon debate begins.
4. **The Violet Jester** — Founders' Night hijack; bridge hostages; three jack-bombs; boss fight on the clock tower. Reveal: Ferrous commissioned the attack and Malloy is back with him. Okafor's last tape: *"Strings only hold if someone chooses to hold on."*
